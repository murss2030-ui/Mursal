# PRECEPTOR — M6 plan and decisions (autonomous execution)

**Date:** 22 October 2026. By the owner's instruction of the same day, M6 onward is built without stopping for approval; where the specification allows more than one reading, the safest is chosen and recorded here and in the report.

**Source note.** The text of the specification's Section 22 for M6–M9 is not in the repository or in this session's record. M6 is built from what the accepted plans and the task catalogue say about it: *"PDF export of the department form (Ward and Teaching copies), blocked while anything is unverified"* (Task 18), *"the PDF export of the form (M6, where export blocking is enforced on the file itself)"* (M4 plan), *"the monitoring due/overdue rule ships with M6"* (M2 decision 8), and *"M6 reconciliation at admission, transfer and discharge; discharge, follow-up, form export"* (M2 plan).

## Decisions

1. **How the PDF is made: the browser's own print to PDF.** No PDF library, no font embedding (Arabic would need shaping), no network: the export screen renders the document and the browser prints it, with the Arabic and Latin fonts the app already bundles. A plain **text file** is offered as a second form. *Safest because:* nothing is generated server-side, nothing can be sent, and Arabic is rendered by the engine that already renders the app.
2. **Blocking on the file itself.** The export is built only from the form's own view (the one on screen), so nothing the form leaves out can reach it; and it is **refused, with the reasons**, while anything shown is unverified: a value read by the model and not confirmed, a model suggestion with no decision, a calculation out of date, a data conflict (NKDA conflict, interpretation needing re-review), a Problem Card held back for a stale re-analysis, undecided, evidence, senior review, an unverified claim, a classification question or an untraced dose, or values from documents still waiting to be checked. A *rejected* card (a final decision) and a card with no final recommendation (only unfinished, listed under "missing") do not block.
3. **Ward copy.** It carries identifiers, so it is refused for a Teaching Case and, until the release gate of Section 23 is met, for every case. The Teaching copy never holds an identifier (verified in tests).
4. **What the file says about itself.** The case id, the copy, the date and version; that it was prepared by a trainee under supervision, that every recommendation was decided by the trainee, that PRECEPTOR sent nothing to the care team, that it does not replace a clinical pharmacist; a SHA-256 of its content. Values keep their provenance in brackets. The supervisor's signature and the date beside it are **lines left blank to fill in by hand** — the date is not typed by the app.
5. **Audit.** Each export (PDF or text) is recorded by its copy, the number of sections and missing items and its hash — never its content.
6. **Monitoring due/overdue** is a rule of the offline engine over the trainee's own plan (Task 11): a planned check day passed is *important*, today is *informational*, no day, no prompt. Across cases it is the existing Workflow counters.
7. **Reconciliation** is a rule over what the trainee recorded in Task 3: a home medication not compared after the first day (*important*), an unintentional discrepancy not resolved (*important*), an intentional change with no documented reason and a recorded transfer (*informational*). The rule compares nothing itself and sends nothing to anyone.
